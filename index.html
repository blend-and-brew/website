        Terms & Conditions

        Blend & Brew · Mobile Coffee Wagon

        Service Hours

        Your package includes a specified number of service hours. Your finish time will depend on the package selected and your preferred service start time.

        Setup and pack-down are provided at no additional cost and are completed outside of your booked service hours.

        Package Inclusions

        Each package includes the number of drinks and the service hours outlined in your booking confirmation.

        If demand exceeds the included quantity during your event, Blend & Brew can continue service with additional drinks available at an additional charge.

        If additional drinks are served beyond your package allowance, they will be charged according to the arrangement selected during your booking process.

        Additional Service Time

        If you would like to extend service beyond your booked hours on the day, please speak to your baristas — extra time is offered subject to availability (including the baristas' next commitment) and is not guaranteed.

        Any extension must be approved on-site by the attending baristas before the additional time begins. Once approved, additional service time will be charged at our standard hourly rate, confirmed with you at the time of approval.

        Payment for additional service time is due either on the day (by card or transfer before the extension begins) or by invoice issued after the event, at Blend & Brew's discretion. Where an invoice is issued, payment is required within seven (7) days of the invoice date. Extra time is not considered confirmed, and is not covered by this booking's insurance or staffing arrangements, until payment has been received in accordance with whichever method is specified at the time.

        Venue Requirements

        To ensure we can deliver a smooth and efficient service, the following must be available at your venue:

        • A minimum of two (2) standard 10A power outlets located within reach of the coffee cart.
        • Access to a loading zone or temporary parking space at, or immediately adjacent to, the venue for approximately 30 minutes before and after the event to allow for setup and pack-down.
        • Ramp or lift access where stairs are present.

        If you're unsure whether your venue can accommodate these requirements, please contact us before confirming your booking — we're happy to help. Blend & Brew may be unable to provide the service as quoted if these venue requirements cannot be met.

        Travel

        Blend & Brew proudly services Western Sydney and surrounding suburbs.

        Events outside our standard service area incur a travel fee based on distance. This fee is calculated and confirmed with you in writing before your booking is finalised, and forms part of your total booking cost from that point.

        Once confirmed, the travel fee is treated the same as the rest of your booking cost for the purposes of our Cancellation policy below — it is not refunded separately, and cancelling your event does not remove or reduce your liability for it if the cancellation falls within the notice period described below.

        Additional Drinks

        Your package includes the agreed number of drinks outlined in your booking confirmation. If the number of drinks served exceeds your package allowance, Blend & Brew may continue service with additional drinks charged accordingly. Additional drinks can either be charged directly to guests during the event or recorded and invoiced to the event host following the event.

        Cancellations

        Bookings may be cancelled up to seven (7) days prior to the event without penalty, and any deposit or payment made will be refunded in full.

        Cancellations received within seven (7) days of the scheduled event will void any right to a refund. This applies to the full booking cost, including any confirmed travel fee, to cover staffing, preparation, administrative costs, and the loss of potential bookings.

        This policy applies regardless of the reason for cancellation, including changes of mind, double-bookings, or events being postponed, cancelled, or moved indoors/outdoors by the client or venue.

        Booking Confirmation

        By proceeding with your booking, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agree to these Terms & Conditions, and that you are authorised to accept this booking and any associated invoice on behalf of the organisation or event.
